  4. Zero-JIT RegExp range, sticky-filter, and greedy-tail scanner (`5.67x–5.93x` V8-v7 RegExp vs Stock NG 0.17.0); owned RegExp replace/flags fast paths.
  5. 256-bit / Curve25519 BigInt kernels (`bigint-noble-ed25519` beats Node 24 `V8 --jitless` on both Linux x86-64 [`1.16x`] and ARM64 [`1.08x`]).
