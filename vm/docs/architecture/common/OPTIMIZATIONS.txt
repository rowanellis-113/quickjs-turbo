Integrated product components
=============================

Use `BUILDING.txt` to build the product configuration on the pinned QuickJS-NG
`0.17.0` (`7b5f588e0f`) public API. Sources are organized strictly under
`vm/{src,asm,tools,cmake,tests}/common | arm64 | x86`. See
`ABI-AND-BASELINE-JIT.txt` for the shipping register-frame ABI contracts and
the proposed future bytecode-driven Baseline JIT roadmap.
