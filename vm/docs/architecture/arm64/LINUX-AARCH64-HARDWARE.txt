Linux AArch64 Hardware Benchmarks, 2026-11-28 (v0.17.0-turbo.4 Hardware Cohort)
===============================================================================
Host: AWS Graviton2 Neoverse-N1 (Ubuntu, GCC 13.4, CPU1 serial, normal ASLR, FramePool 256).
Hardware cohort release: `v0.17.0-turbo.4` (commit `6ed860a`, 2026-11-28).
Measured `v0.17.0-turbo.4` ARM64 binary SHA-256:
  `91c2ed014d0fcbc854ea3edd8e886cb8b52c66b61618f20b248e80fe6670416f`
  (reproduced byte-for-byte from a clean `git archive 6ed860a` checkout).

Cohort scope:
- Cohort A (4-engine equal-path ABBA on CPU1): Stock QuickJS-NG `0.17.0` (`7b5f588e0f`),
  Bellard QuickJS `2026-06-04` (`-O2`), `v0.17.0-turbo.3` (`turbo3_arm`), and
  `v0.17.0-turbo.4` (`turbo4_arm`: Waves A1–A3 zero-spill native AArch64 CVM executor,
  widened modern-JS admission + zero-exit try/catch ASM, Tier-5 VA constructor compiler,
  inline compound-leaf hot paths, zero-JIT RegExp scanner, and 256-bit/Curve25519 BigInt).
- Cohort B (paired same-host ABBA on CPU1): `v0.17.0-turbo.4` (`turbo4_arm`) vs
  official `Node.js v24.13.0 --jitless` (Linux `aarch64`).
