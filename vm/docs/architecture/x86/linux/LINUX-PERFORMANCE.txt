Linux x86-64 measured development runtime: performance and cost review
=====================================================================

The runtime is4d8363a,documented at1109aa0 and qualified at90e3bf3. Independent
canonical LF archive builds produce complete native and PORTABLE CLI,Test262
runner and static-library bytes identical to the qualified/measured images.
These scores bind to those installed Linux images;they are not new benchmark
runs. Stock is unmodified NG0.15.1 bf8988f,not Bellard2026. LINUX-RELEASE.txt
records current qualification and hashes.

All three images used original immutable calibration/workloads, CPU2 serial
forward/reverse pairs, normal PIE/ASLR and allocator setting256. No PGO, image
base selection, padding or address search was used. This first complete screen
is a filter, not a confidence interval or a guarantee for every application.
The JetStream column is the retained17-case subset, not an official total score.

observations identify generator/legacy/native boundaries and slow-path/reload
costs as investigation areas. Self-samples are not call counts or removable-time
estimates. Repeated profile bodies do not replace original calibrated scores.

prop_delete has multiple populations: selected native43.94..111.6ns/op with
different final calibration N. Do not turn its first117% screen delta into an
intrinsic delete-opcode regression or blame ASLR. float_to_string's initial
PORTABLE86.2ns sample did not recur in selected confirmation (330.335ns median);
native367.585 vs stock363.0 is not a stable fourfold regression. string_concat1's
apparent PORTABLE regression also did not persist. All original samples remain
in the external audit, including negative results and final calibration N.

Bounded Linux opportunity decisions
----------------------------------
SysV aggregate return/arguments and helper preservation are already implemented
using actual compiler types; there is no missing Win64 hidden-result-pointer
conversion to optimize. Current engine calls are direct/local with zero engine
function JUMP_SLOT relocations. No PLT-bypass experiment was justified.

A measured short-callback policy was tested on a separate branch62f5fb2:
small normal callbacks under legacy synchronous generators retained original C
execution. Same-image ON/OFF and natural original-caller observations proved
the intended route, and927CTest, three fullTC modes and public GC/reentry/
capture/exception/cleanup checks passed. Complete original suite screening gave
lazy~8.2%better, micro overall+0.22%time and JetStream-0.08%time (effectively flat),
while cdjs/richards added~4.6/5.0%. V8's quick+2.90%time change was uncertain
because the accepted DeltaBlue scores included1785/1221 modes. The candidate
did not demonstrate enough net benefit to justify a new heuristic/control, and
is NOT integrated. No candidate runtime source/cache/control remains in product.

Native generator execution and broader fast-opcode coverage remain possible
future features with a larger implementation/maintenance cost. This bounded
decision is not proof of an architectural ceiling. Default-active GNU transfer
coverage is closed independently of performance. Individual historical bests
are not release requirements; price each change by both prior and stock time.
